What the application does

Select a local video, choose an authorized channel, enter its title and description, and select a submission action. The application uses the official YouTube API to upload the file and checks the resulting channel, visibility, and processing status.

  • Save an uploaded video as private, or submit it with the visibility you selected.
  • Keep local task records and resume an interrupted upload using its existing session.
  • Read video statistics and, when authorized, YouTube Analytics reports such as views and watch time.

An optional browser workflow can use Codex and a signed-in browser instead of API submission. Video Release also includes a separate browser workflow for Douyin. These workflows do not receive authority to other accounts through your Google OAuth grant.

Why Google access is requested

Manage your YouTube videos

The youtube.upload scope allows the application to upload a selected video with its title, description, audience declaration, AI content declaration, and requested visibility.

View your YouTube account

The youtube.readonly scope identifies the authorized channel and reads video information, processing results, and supported statistics.

Read YouTube Analytics reports

The yt-analytics.readonly scope permits read-only access to supported channel and video reports. The application does not request monetary Analytics access.

Where it runs

The publisher runs on your computer. This website is an informational site; it does not accept video uploads, collect your Google password, or host the publishing service. Video upload requests and OAuth token exchanges are sent to Google and YouTube.

You choose the channel and submission action. A private upload is a private YouTube video, not a YouTube Studio draft. YouTube processing, content rules, quota, and project restrictions still apply.
